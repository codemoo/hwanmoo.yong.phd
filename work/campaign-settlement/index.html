Campaign settlement platform

Development·2026–now·Platform architecture, backend and operations

Customers fund campaigns, many independent participants carry them out, and this platform settles the money between them. Every approved task ends in a payout, so each submission has to be checked before money moves. Manual review cannot keep up as volume grows, and every wrong approval is money out the door.

I designed the platform around a single settlement engine and led its backend development. A participant app, a self-serve customer portal and an internal admin tool all run on that engine. Before any payout, the platform reads and verifies the submitted evidence automatically.

Every movement of money is recorded in one double-entry ledger, where entries are only ever added, never edited. Any balance traces back to the exact transactions behind it. An integrity audit cross-checks balances against those transactions.

The same engine later grew to support a multi-tier partner network that resells campaigns. The platform is live, and every payout to participants settles through that one ledger.
